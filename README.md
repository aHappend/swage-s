**Swage-S** is the vision and image signal processing (ISP) series within Vulcan's **Swage** silicon family, transforming raw sensor and frame streams into target geometric and color representations through line-buffered streaming pipelines (`SWAGE-S1` -> `SWAGE-S2` -> `SWAGE-S3`).

## Silicon Signoff Matrix & Dual-Track Publications (`100 MHz+` Baseline)

| Gen | Accelerator | Architecture & Numerical Scope | Quartus 17.1 STA (`10.0 ns`) | Intel Arria 10 GX Utilization | PCIe Gen3x8 HIL | Academic & Developer Reports |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gen-1** | **SWAGE-S1** | Streaming Bilinear & Nearest-Neighbor 2D Image Scaler · Dual-Line SRAM Buffer & Q8.8/Q16.16 Phase Accumulators (`5 modules, 469 LOC`) | Target `>= 100 MHz` | — | Scheduled | *In Active Vulcan Verification* |
| **Gen-2** | **SWAGE-S2** | Streaming 2D Scaler + Programmable 3×3 Spatial Convolution Filter (Sobel / Gaussian / Sharpen) with Line-Buffer Reuse | Target `>= 100 MHz` | — | Scheduled | *Queued in Vulcan Daemon* |
| **Gen-3** | **SWAGE-S3** | Full Streaming ISP Pipeline · Bayer Demosaicing + Programmable RGB/YCbCr Color-Space Conversion + 3×3 Filter + 2D Scaler | Target `>= 100 MHz` | — | Scheduled | *Queued in Vulcan Daemon* |
